operational changes and Service Manager approval, not a wording-only fix.

## COM-02 - Approval limits

The Commercial Director may discount only the subscription line by at most
10%, not the whole first-year quote. Larger concessions need CFO approval.

computed without delivery and operating costs. Standard offer validity is
90 days, not 120.

## LEG-01 - Negotiation guardrails

The standard liability cap is 12 months of fees. Unlimited liability, uncapped
penalties, broad IP transfer and acceptance without objective tests require
legal/executive review if actually requested. Do not invent those buyer terms.
Payment preference is 40% kickoff, 40% UAT and 20% acceptance; negotiable
milestone terms alone are not proof of a conflict.
