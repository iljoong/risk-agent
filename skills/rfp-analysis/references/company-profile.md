## COMP-01 - Business and offering

Cedarbridge Digital Services is a fictional 42-person B2B software and delivery
company. Its product, Cedarbridge Knowledge Hub, provides governed document
search for internal operations teams. Its strongest experience is deployment
for organizations with 50-200 named users and text-based PDF collections.

## COMP-02 - Established strengths

The standard product supports reader/editor/administrator roles, separate
regional workspaces, document owners and effective-date metadata, keyword and
semantic search, and document/page citations. Content and metadata can be
exported as PDF files plus CSV/JSON. These are documented synthetic capabilities,
not independently tested claims about a real product.

## COMP-03 - Scope and evidence boundaries

mobile apps. English is supported. Korean localization is a roadmap item with
no committed delivery date. No partner has committed to fill capability gaps.
This pack supplies an invented internal baseline, not actual audit certificates,
