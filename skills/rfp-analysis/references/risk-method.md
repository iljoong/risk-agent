# Proposal risk method

SYNTHETIC TEST GUIDANCE. Use for simulation, not as legal or financial advice.

## METHOD-01 - Scoring

Score likelihood and impact separately from 1 to 5. Likelihood: 1 remote,
2 unlikely, 3 plausible, 4 likely, 5 existing/near-certain gap. Impact: 1 minor,
2 manageable, 3 material rework/cost, 4 major service/commercial exposure,
5 disqualification, serious data exposure or inability to deliver.
Score = likelihood x impact: 20-25 Critical, 12-19 High, 6-11 Medium, 1-5 Low.
Explain judgments; the numbers are not measured probabilities or expected loss.

## METHOD-02 - Mandatory gates

A known unmet mandatory condition remains a blocker regardless of score.
Missing proof is "Needs confirmation", not automatically proven failure or fit.
Distinguish mandatory requirements from desirable features, aspirational targets
and negotiated terms. Keep unverified mitigations separate from current state.
Do not lower residual risk unless the mitigation's dependency and approval
are explicit; otherwise label residual risk provisional.

## METHOD-03 - Recommendation

- Bid: mandatory conditions have support and material risks are acceptable.
- Conditional bid: credible, identified fixes or buyer concessions can resolve
  blockers before the required decision milestone; approvals are still pending.
- No bid as-is: known mandatory blockers remain without a credible approved
  resolution in time. This is an advisory recommendation, not an approval.
- Insufficient evidence: the source coverage cannot support a defensible choice.

Do not manufacture numerical risk counts or claim an exhaustive review.

## METHOD-04 - Financial comparisons and citations

Compare total required base costs with a hard first-year cap on like-for-like
currency/tax/scope. Treat a renewal target separately. Apply discounts only
to the allowed line items. Show arithmetic and retain unpriced changes.
Every material risk needs a buyer/proposal source plus a supplier fact ID.
Use PDF filename/page/requirement IDs and reference filename/fact IDs; do not
invent page numbers for Markdown or claim supplied internal notes are audits.
